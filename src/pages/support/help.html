Help Center

Find your answer quickly.

Learn how ACTUAL HOLDINGS accounts, services, orders, documents and support work.

How do I create an account?

Select Sign Up, provide the requested information and verify your account. Your dashboard becomes the place to manage requests, orders and messages.

How do I request a service?

Explore Services, choose the service you need and sign in when you are ready to submit a request. The request can then be tracked from your dashboard.

How does Shop work?

Browse products, review details, add items to your cart and continue to checkout. Order and payment status can be connected to your account.

What about my documents?

Documents are handled through authenticated areas and controlled access. Do not send passwords, PINs or other credentials through public forms.

How can I contact the team?

Use Contact for general inquiries or the authenticated messaging area when you have an active service or account.

How is access controlled?

The platform is designed around authentication, role-based permissions, server-side authorization and activity monitoring.

PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

ACTUAL HOLDINGS CO LTD aims to collect only the information needed to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, manage client accounts and support orders. Information may include contact details, business details, service instructions and documents that a client chooses to submit.

How information is used

Information is used to communicate with clients, assess and deliver requested services, process orders, verify payments, provide support and protect platform operations. Access should be limited to authorized personnel who need the information for their work.

Documents and account security

Do not submit passwords, PINs, one-time verification codes or unrelated sensitive information through public forms. Secure document upload and server-side access controls must be enabled before confidential documents are submitted through a production account.

Retention and contact

Records should be retained only as required for legitimate business, legal and operational purposes. To ask about personal information or request a correction, contact ACTUAL HOLDINGS through the Contact page. This policy text is a frontend draft and should be reviewed for applicable Rwandan legal requirements before launch.
