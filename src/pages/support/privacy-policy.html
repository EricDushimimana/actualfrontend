TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

This page describes the intended privacy approach for the ACTUAL HOLDINGS website and frontend preview. It must be reviewed and finalized by the company before production use.

Information you provide

When you contact ACTUAL HOLDINGS or use a form, you may provide details such as your name, contact information, business details and the information needed to respond to your request. Do not submit passwords, payment card details or highly sensitive documents through this frontend preview.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, assess service requests, communicate about orders and improve customer support. The live service must document the lawful basis, retention periods and responsible data controller before launch.

Storage and security

This frontend preview may store demo preferences or sample records in your browser. It is not a secure production database. Secure accounts, access controls, document storage, backups and audit logging require the PHP/MySQL backend and appropriate server-side security.

Cookies and local preferences

The website may store a local preference to remember whether you dismissed the privacy notice. This preview does not use that notice to claim that analytics or advertising cookies are active.

Your choices and contact

For questions about information you have submitted, contact ACTUAL HOLDINGS through the Contact page. The company should publish its verified contact details and data-rights process here before production launch.
